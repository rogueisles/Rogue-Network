PROOF AND CONFIGURATION RULES
Canonical Scrypt only after activation; private CPU target scaling is rejected.
V3 multi proofs must include DGB, DOGE or both. Litecoin/other anchors are not
enabled in this release. Missing/old proofs, zero/disabled participation, altered
bindings, insufficient or duplicate quorum signatures and invalid work reject.
The template signatures attest native proposal bindings; they do not replace
proof-of-work validation or prove native liveness independently at every peer.
RetargetV2 remains unchanged, with configured target 12 seconds. The user accepts
the recorded timing; no new timing tuning or exact 12-second claim is made.
Production public signer identities, independently validating services, quorum
and difficulty still require a configuration freeze before deployment. Local
ephemeral signer keys are not production identities. An ordinary restart retains
the explicitly installed configuration; incompatible active changes fail startup.

RPC AND POOL
Existing eth_getBlockReward and minerReward fields remain. Total is normal
subsidy only; specialIssuance is additional treasury issuance. New fields include
p2e, treasury and currencySymbol. Tribe/Maintenance are zero under the new split.
The pool consumes canonical miner subsidy plus its existing transaction fee
policy, excluding special issuance. Normal rewards become zero after exhaustion;
transaction fees remain eligible under that same policy.
For adoption, pool forgeBlock is 1100100 and both pool/signers explicitly use
externalChainPolicy="dgb-doge". Internal OGG Redis/round identifiers remain.

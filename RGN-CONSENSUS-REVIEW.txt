CONSENSUS / SECURITY REVIEW -- RGN MAINNET SOURCE CANDIDATE
20 October 2026

Review scope
Review of candidate changes against the actual original mainnet source and
retained D14 source, supplemented by targeted and broader executable checks.
This is an engineering qualification by the implementing assistant, not an
independent external security audit or a proof that no vulnerabilities exist.

Resolved finding: active-config auto-rewind
The inherited Ethereum startup accepts a bare ConfigCompatError, rewinds to its
suggested height, and writes the replacement configuration. The initial RGN
configuration tests checked rejection inside SetupGenesisBlock but did not
account for that caller behavior. A new regression failed on the old behavior.
SetupGenesisBlock now returns a wrapped terminal error when a configuration
conflicts with an already active RGN schedule (or installs a mismatched active
RGN schedule over imported history). Full/light-node callers stop instead of
automatically rewinding. Tests cover removal, proof-parameter mutation, moved
activation, changed historical rules, unchanged stored config and head, and
ordinary nil-genesis restart. Pre-H and non-RGN handling are preserved.

Historical invariants
The exact original mainnet genesis and old EVM rules remain. The candidate's
ProgPoW kernel and old difficulty eras preserve the D14 compatibility path.
The legacy reward optimization returns an integer only when independently
rounded lower/upper bounds certify it; exact rational fallback is retained.
All historical reward heights through H-1 are summed to the frozen gross
issuance snapshot, including genesis and the old Tribe seed.
Full historical block/state/receipt/seal replay is reported separately with a
fixed independently cross-checked checkpoint. The future unknown gap between
that checkpoint and H is not represented as existing historical evidence.

Reward invariants
Normal rewards, special treasury credits and inherited fees are distinct.
The approved v2 schedule uses option B Q36 decay until its curve reaches
100 RGN, then pays a finite 100 RGN tail with one final partial reward. Two
independent exhaustive interval recurrences certify the exact floored prefix;
that prefix plus all tail rewards equals the normal allowance exactly. The
height-only subtraction cannot reopen an allowance after spending or burning.
The split conserves every wei with dust to the miner. Special issuance depends
only on canonical height, with no mutable paid flag, wall clock or cron task.
The persisted-chain rehearsals cover canonical replacement and reapplication
of the initial credit, restart, every monthly boundary and absent 37th release,
plus tail onset, final partial reward and zero subsidy after cap exhaustion.
The pool consumes only the node's canonical miner-reward field and preserves
the installed variable-difficulty accounting behavior.

Proof invariants
The Hybrid engine selects the old/new rules at the exact inclusive height.
Mixed batches use already-verified header overlays across the boundary.
Mainnet rejects private CPU target scaling, old/missing proofs and disabled
external participation. RGN permits DGB-only, DOGE-only or both; zero-anchor or
bonus-only work cannot satisfy the profile. V3 binds the signed external body
to the exact RGN work hash; native body/branch, treasury scripts, auxiliary
identities, canonical Merkle tree and signer quorum are checked. Envelope and
branch sizes are bounded. Canonical Scrypt work is checked separately from
the attested native-template bindings.

Remaining review/qualification limits
- The four peer nodes verify signed external-template attestations; they do
  not independently prove native-chain liveness by querying DGB/DOGE in the
  consensus path. Signer custody, independent native validation and quorum
  availability are real production trust assumptions. One signer is a single
  attestation availability/trust point. Its final mainnet policy is not frozen.
- RetargetV2 is a custom relative exponential rule carried from D14. It is not
  BCH ASERT consensus. Boundary, overflow, direction, timestamp and independent
  exponential-reference tests help qualification; independent review remains.
- The inherited total-difficulty fork choice crosses different historical and
  future proof domains. No new checkpoint or chainwork conversion has been
  silently introduced. Deep reorganisation economics near a proof-algorithm
  change deserve explicit independent review before production activation.
- Persisted boundary rehearsals deliberately skip seals in a TEST-ONLY engine
  wrapper. They validate headers/difficulty/body/EVM/receipts/state/reorg/restart,
  not a newly mined canonical-target RGN activation proof. Native private-lab
  proof matrices validate bindings at private difficulty and are labelled so.
- The previous v1 isolated real-miner sample passed canonical-target sealing
  and state execution from a trusted synthetic H-1 through H+11. Its unchanged
  first-H block was rechecked byte for byte under v2, with all 12 invalid proof
  variants rejected, full state import and restart. Later v1 reward roots are
  not evidence of the changed v2 curve. Current v2 persisted state/tail/cap
  tests passed. Production signer identities remain unfrozen; the local
  signers share native backends.
- The earlier sparse-checkpoint pruning crash left four uncertain OGG sends.
  Archive mode corrects that experiment's storage setup. The exact four proofs
  were reconstructed and fully imported. A copy of the actual saved six-record
  journal, node database and Redis on PRIMARY passed positive reconciliation,
  ordered replay and restarts without duplicate share credit or candidates.
  Original data and existing services stayed unchanged. This is fixture-specific
  accounting recovery, not a general missing-candidate-body recovery service.
  Production review should address durable retention/recovery of complete issued
  candidates and distinguish RPC acknowledgement from completed block storage.
- Broader core/consensus/eth/miner tests do not imply every inherited subsystem
  or every third-party dependency has a clean independent security audit.
- Existing staking/Tribe bytecode and storage are preserved. These preservation
  checks do not independently audit contract-specific yield/claim formulas or
  their long-term funding assumptions under the new staking allocation.

No live mainnet consensus, source checkout, executable, account, wallet or
service was replaced during this qualification. Original-binary reproductions
used isolated artifact folders; the original source/deployed hashes stayed fixed.

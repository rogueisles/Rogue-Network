WHAT CHANGED AFTER THE EARLIER UPLOAD
1. A mined certificate binds the exact template, work and quorum signatures into
   the parent coinbase. Altering authorization cannot create another block identity
   using the same unchanged mined parent. V4 is required by the new profile.
2. Activated fork choice uses target-derived work consistently in block/header/
   receipt import, unavailable-state recovery and peer messages. Historical work
   increments remain unchanged. This fixes incompatible raw-difficulty arithmetic.
3. Signer limits and exact ordered, distinct quorum signatures prevent oversized
   or ambiguous certificates. The maximum configured signer count is 16.
4. Activated body checks precede both known-block and unavailable-parent-state
   shortcuts. Invalid transaction/uncle bodies cannot bypass checks after pruning
   or rollback. Historical validation ordering is preserved before activation.
5. The custom eth_getBlockReward endpoint retains old amount/address fields and
   reports height-selected allocations, new P2E/treasury destinations and separate
   special issuance. internal/ethapi/ogg_api.go is updated, not deleted: it holds
   the response definition used by the registered method in eth/api.go. There is
   no second independent reward calculation or competing RPC handler.
6. The companion pool and signer handle the issuer's proofVersion, reconstruct V4
   native proposals and bind the signed certificate before publishing Stratum jobs.
   Saved submission intents retain the exact proof across lost replies/restarts.
7. The profile identifier changes from rgn-mainnet-v2 to rgn-mainnet-v3. The approved
   Option B multiplier, capped 100 RGN tail, reward split and 10.7B cap do not change.
